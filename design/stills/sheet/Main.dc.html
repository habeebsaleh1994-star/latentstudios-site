Latent Studios
Stills · the world, first try · 3 September 2026
Two rooms: the room, and the road.
Ten frames from the interview, made with image models, no photograph of ours in them. The first seven are the room, where the image is developed: Barry Lyndon and the archives. The last three are the road, where the image is born: Kiarostami. They test one thing: whether this is the world. Candlelight and window light, paper and its fibre, engravings, sketches, textiles, and inside them the studio's own forms: the image born before the act, the negative, the sheet turning, the seam, the frame.
1 · The image, born before the act
A sheet on a table by candlelight, and a tree already in it, faint, as if it had always been there. This is the belief in one frame. The bare version, no window, no plaque, is the stronger one.
1, second take · with a window
Same idea in daylight. The museum plaque is a mistake the model made, and a lesson: the world should never label itself. Kept for the light, which is right.
2 · The negative over the plate
An engraving of leaves and, laid over it, their negative in silver. The archive and the photograph as one object. This is the Ritual's whole argument without a screenshot.
3 · The leaf, turning
A sketch on the recto, a line on the verso, caught in the air. Versos, and the book. The candlelit room behind is Barry Lyndon's.
4 · Silver, growing on the archive
The material of photography crystallising over a botanical plate. Texture and imperfection carried with elegance. This is what grain should look like in the film: structure, not specks.
5 · The seam
Candlelight and daylight in one room, divided by a window. Raw and developed, before and after, as light. The gilt frame the model added is another lesson: the archive doesn't need a frame around it. Ours is the only frame.
6 · The frame among the archive
The mark, drawn in ink on paper, open at one corner, with an engraving, a red-chalk figure, batik and a glass negative around it. The studio's one hard edge inside the soft world. The film ends here.
What the seven say
The world holds. Warm without being dark, archival without being dusty, and none of it looks like software. Two corrections for the next pass: never let the image label itself, and never let it frame itself. What's missing on purpose: any product, any screen, any caption.
If this is right, the film is these frames coming alive, and the site is the room they hang in.
The road
Where the image is born · Kiarostami
7 · The road
A dirt road curving over a bare hill into white haze. Three lines: road, ridge, sky. Held for as long as you can stand it. The film opens here.
8 · The tree
One tree in snow, a single track crossing the field. It is the engraved tree from the candlelit sheet, alive and outside. The two rooms share it.
9 · The window
An olive grove through a dusty windshield, the road ahead empty. The frame as a window on the world before it is a print. Ordinary and sacred at once.
