The mark · six directions from the interview · drawn, not generated

A mark that was born before it was drawn.

The frame we had was drawn before the interview, and it shows: it is a picture of a print. These six come from sentences you said. Each is shown large, small, and beside the name. None is finished. The point is to find which idea is the mark, then draw that one until it is inevitable.

Latent Studios

A · Prior

"Born before the act."

A point, and the bloom before it: halation around something that has not yet become anything. The thought, and the glow it casts before it is thought. The only mark here with no edge at all.

Risk: soft marks are hard at favicon size; the small version becomes a dot in a ring. Also close to a sun, an eye, a planet.

Latent Studios

B · The thought about the thought

"When you think of a thought, you have a thought about the thought."

A spiral of open circles, each one beginning again inside the last, each fainter: the same becoming, smaller, never closing. Recursion drawn as one gesture.

Risk: spirals are common (and the Moment's ring is a cousin). Would need a distinctive break in the line to be its own.

Latent Studios

C · Two extremes

"It joins two extremes, and in what is between them it plays."

A form that begins as nothing on the left and arrives as a hard edge on the right, with everything between. Before the world at one end, the real thing at the other. The edge is the studio: where the latent becomes manifest.

Risk: a gradient is hard to reproduce in one colour, in embroidery, in a stamp. Would need a drawn version made of strokes instead of a fade.

Latent Studios

D · Forcing

"Some things grow toward what they cannot see, and the growing is not separate from the sweetness."

From your book: the plant raised in the dark, reaching toward an absent sun. Three strokes rising from one root, the tallest reaching for a light that is only a faint point. The studio as the one who tends the growth.

Risk: reads as a plant, a flame, or a sprout, which are gentle but common. Its strength is the story behind it, which only you would know.

Latent Studios

E · The negative

"Shoot negative, and print."

The world inverted: a dark field, a light disc, a dark centre. Read one way it is a negative of a point of light; read the other, a pupil. The refusal of the finished positive, made into a glyph. Strong at every size.

Risk: the most "logo-like" of the six, and the closest to a camera company's mark. It says photography loudly, and the studio is more than photography.

Latent Studios

F · Prior to the words

"We are prior to our words."

Three lines of writing, fading upward toward a single point above them: the text becoming less and less, until what remains is the silence before the first syllable. A manuscript read backwards to its origin. The most literary of the six, and the one that belongs to both the book and the studio.

Risk: could read as a menu icon or a text-alignment symbol at small size. The point above the lines is what saves it, and it must survive the favicon.

Two of these I would put money on: C, because it is the whole interview in one shape, nothing becoming a hard edge with the play between; and F, because it is yours in a way no one could copy. A and D are the most beautiful and the least usable small. E is the strongest and the least true. B is a cousin of a mark you already have.
