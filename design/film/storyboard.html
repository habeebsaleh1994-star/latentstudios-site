Latent Studios
The film · storyboard · drawn before anything is made

The creation of the world, as the birth of one thought.

Sixty seconds. Nothing whole, nothing named, until the very end. Each drawing is a note toward a shot, not the shot: the film will be made of materials and light, and these are only the rhythm and the intention on paper.

Ink on paper. Tint marks the light of each movement. Duration and sound under every frame.

I · Nothing

Before anything. The audience that did not yet exist.

1 · Before0:00 – 0:05
Not black. A deep grey with the faintest movement, like the inside of closed eyes. No edges.
nothing, then the room
2 · The first warmth0:05 – 0:09
Somewhere in the dark, a warmth. Not a light, the memory of one. Amber, without a source. It breathes once.
a single low tone, far away
3 · First light0:09 – 0:15
The warmth breaks. Light leaking through something like gelatin, halation blooming, the first grain. It never becomes a shape.
the tone opens into a breath

II · Everything

What was waiting. Faster and faster, toward the pulse.

4 · Matter0:15 – 0:20
Grain thickens into structure: silver crystallising, branching, reaching. Then it softens before it can be called frost.
ticks and creaks, the sound of growing
5 · Water and dye0:20 – 0:24
The structure dissolves into liquid: colour soaking into fibre, rose and ochre and indigo feathering outward.
water, close
6 · A pulse begins0:24 – 0:27
The washes begin to beat, slowly, like a heart heard from inside. Each beat brings a glimpse.
the pulse, felt more than heard
7 · Everything that led here0:27 – 0:33
On each beat, faster each time, a fragment of the inspiration folder comes alive for a fraction of a second and dissolves into the next. Never held long enough to name. By the end, several at once.
the pulse quickening into a rhythm, a rising chord beneath
8 · The thought about the thought0:33 – 0:36
The fragments fold inward: the image contains itself, the same becoming beginning again inside, smaller, then inside that.
everything at once, then cut
9 · Prior to the words0:36 – 0:39
Hard cut to the dark of shot 1. Nothing moves. Three seconds, which in a film is a very long time.
absolute silence

IV · The thought   ·   V · The world

Born, with everything behind it. Then a world with an edge.

10 · Born0:39 – 0:52
One shot, slow, unbroken. From a single point, presence arrives: colour and texture and warmth spreading to fill the frame. Almost clear at the end, and stopping just short.
silence, then one held warm note arriving with the colour
11 · The frame0:52 – 0:57
The colour settles into paper. The frame draws itself, open at one corner: the first and only hard edge, fully sharp.
the note fades; paper
12 · The name0:57 – 1:00
Latent Studios, beneath the frame. Then, small, one line from the founder's book. Cut to paper.
nothing
