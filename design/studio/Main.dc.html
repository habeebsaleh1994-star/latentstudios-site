One engine, one habit
The Moment catches. The Ritual lets you sit with the work. The Lab carries the process through motion. Versos hands one photograph to someone who looks.
Different things, one habit. A developer that reveals instead of corrects. A wall where a person looks instead of a machine. Slow down, look again, let the photograph say what it already knows.
The shared world

Keep them in a Circle, or place them in the Field. Once a month, hang one on the wall. The rest belongs to whoever stops to look.
