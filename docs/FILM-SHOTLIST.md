# Latent Studios, the film: shot list

4 September 2026. Sixty seconds. The creation of the world, told as the
birth of one thought. Nothing shot with a camera, because it cannot be.

## The form, before the shots

- **Nothing is held.** The instant an image is clear enough to name, it
  is already becoming the next. Each shot is the thought about the shot
  before it. Technically: every clip is seeded from the last frame of the
  previous clip, so the film is one continuous becoming, not a sequence
  of things. Cuts happen only where the story cuts (the hold).
- **Clear and not clear at once.** Focus breathes: it resolves toward
  something and dissolves before it arrives. Nothing is ever fully sharp
  except the frame at the end.
- **Recursion.** Twice in the film, the image contains itself: a form
  inside which the same form is beginning again, smaller. A thought
  about a thought.
- **Nothing whole.** No object, place, face, instrument or picture can
  be named. If a viewer can say "that is a hen", the shot is cut or
  shortened until they cannot.

## The shots

Times are targets. Each shot names its image, its motion, its sound,
and what it means. Durations shorten as the film accelerates.

### I. Nothing (0:00 to 0:09)

**1 · 0:00 to 0:05 · Before.** Not black. A deep, dark grey with the
faintest movement in it, like the inside of closed eyes. No edges.
*Sound:* nothing, then the room. *Meaning:* before anything; the
audience that did not yet exist.

**2 · 0:05 to 0:09 · The first warmth.** Somewhere in the dark, a
warmth begins, not a light, the memory of one, amber, without a source.
It breathes once. *Sound:* a single low tone, far away. *Meaning:* the
need to be seen that preceded seeing.

### II. Everything (0:09 to 0:36)

**3 · 0:09 to 0:15 · First light.** The warmth breaks. A burst that is
not an explosion: light leaking outward through something like gelatin,
halation blooming, the first grain. The image never resolves into a
shape. *Sound:* the tone opens into a breath. *Meaning:* the big bang
as a feeling.

**4 · 0:15 to 0:20 · Matter.** Grain thickens into structure: silver
crystallising, branching, reaching (from still 4, seeded from shot 3's
last frame). Then it softens before it can be called frost. *Sound:*
ticks and creaks, the sound of growing. *Meaning:* things gathering.

**5 · 0:20 to 0:24 · Water and dye.** The structure dissolves into
liquid: colour soaking into fibre, rose and ochre and indigo feathering
outward (from still 12 and 13). *Sound:* water, close. *Meaning:* life.

**6 · 0:24 to 0:27 · A pulse begins.** The washes begin to beat, slowly,
like a heart heard from inside. Each beat brings a glimpse. *Sound:* the
pulse, felt more than heard. *Meaning:* the womb; the first history.

**7 · 0:27 to 0:33 · Everything that led here.** On each beat, faster
each time, a fragment of the archive comes alive for a fraction of a
second and dissolves into the next: a sky, a textile, a garden, a hen, a
marbled endpaper, a figure, a bust, a shore, a ruin at sunset, a road,
one tree in snow, a hand on a page. Never held long enough to name. By
the end they overlap, several at once. *Sound:* the pulse quickening
into a rhythm, and under it a rising chord of every earlier sound.
*Meaning:* the whole history of a thing leading to its present moment;
the world's images, born before anyone made them.

**8 · 0:33 to 0:36 · The thought about the thought.** The overlapping
fragments fold inward: the image contains itself, the same becoming
beginning again inside, smaller, then inside that. Recursion, twice.
The pulse is almost continuous. *Sound:* everything at once, then
cut. *Meaning:* a thought thinking about itself; clear and not clear.

### III. Now (0:36 to 0:39)

**9 · 0:36 to 0:39 · Prior to the words.** Hard cut to the dark of shot
1. Nothing moves. Absolute silence for three seconds, which in a film is
a very long time. *Meaning:* the instant before the first syllable.

### IV. The thought (0:39 to 0:52)

**10 · 0:39 to 0:52 · Born.** One shot, slow, unbroken. From a single
point, presence arrives: colour and texture and warmth spreading to
fill the frame, the register of still 12 exactly, cream, rose, ochre,
sage, warm silver, alive and tactile and not a picture of anything. As
it fills, for the last three seconds, focus resolves further than it
has anywhere in the film, almost to clarity, and stops just short.
*Sound:* silence, then one held warm note, arriving with the colour.
*Meaning:* the thought is born, with everything behind it. Sweeter for
the dark.

### V. The world (0:52 to 1:00)

**11 · 0:52 to 0:57 · The frame.** The colour settles into paper. The
frame draws itself, open at one corner: the first and only hard edge,
fully sharp. *Sound:* the note fades; paper. *Meaning:* a world with an
edge, inside which the arts happen. The studio.

**12 · 0:57 to 1:00 · The name.** Latent Studios, beneath the frame.
Then, small, one line from the founder's book: *We are prior to our
words.* Cut to paper. *Sound:* nothing.

## What each shot is made from

| Shot | Source | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1, 2, 9 | code | shader: dark field, breathing warmth |
| 3 | generated | image-to-video from a new still: light through gelatin, halation, first grain |
| 4 | generated | still 4 (silver), seeded from shot 3's last frame |
| 5 | generated | stills 12 and 13 (dye), seeded from shot 4's last frame |
| 6 | code + generated | shot 5's clip, pulsed in code |
| 7 | generated | the inspiration folder, image-to-video, 2 to 3 seconds each, cut and overlapped in code |
| 8 | code | recursion composited in code from shot 7's material |
| 10 | generated | still 12, one long take on the best model, three attempts |
| 11, 12 | code | the frame and the words, already built |

Every generated clip is made from the last frame of the one before it,
so nothing is ever a cut inside a movement. The film is one becoming.

## Sound, as a line

Room, then a low tone, then a breath, then ticks and creaks of growth,
then water, then a pulse that quickens into a rhythm under a rising
chord, then absolute silence, then one held note, then paper, then
nothing. No music in the usual sense at any point.

## Budget

About 110 seconds of generated video: shots 3 to 5 with two takes each,
the archive fragments once, shot 10 three times on the best model. Well
inside the month.

## The rule that decides every argument

If it can be named, it is not yet the film. If it can be held, it has
stopped being a thought.
