# Latent Studios, the site: a brief for design exploration

For Claude Design or any designer. Written 3 September 2026 by Claude with
Habib. Words are settled (docs/HOMEPAGE-WORDS.md); this is about the look.

## What it is

The home of an independent photographic studio in Beirut, founded by a
photographer. It makes three instruments for developing photographs (the
Ritual on Mac and iPad, the Moment on iPhone, the Lab in DaVinci Resolve)
and a monthly photography prize, Versos. More things will come. The site
must present the studio, not any one product, and give each product its
own room.

## What it must feel like

Beautiful, elegant, harmonious. Calm. A lit room where photographs are
looked at. Serious without being grim. Not a tech company, not a portfolio,
not a template.

Two references, both Habib's own, both to be studied and not copied:

1. **habibsaleh.com**, a digital book. Warm paper (#fbfaf6), Georgia, ink
   (#2a2724), half-pixel hairlines, small-caps sans labels, roman numerals,
   generous space, a bookmark that remembers where you were. Its power is
   that it has a concept (a living manuscript with movements and leaves)
   and every detail serves it. This is the closest thing to what Latent
   Studios should feel like.
2. **versos.latentstudios.art**, the prize. Paper (#f3efe6), Cormorant
   Garamond, a wall of prints with names, words on the back of the print.
   Light and quiet. Habib likes this more than the current studio site.

What to leave behind: the current darkroom site (near-black, amber glow,
full-bleed hero, frosted bars). It is right for the Ritual, which is a
darkroom, and wrong for the studio.

## The rule that organises everything

One source, several rooms, each with its own light.

- Latent Studios is the source: paper, a serif, the frame mark, the calm.
- The Ritual keeps its darkroom (near-black, amber).
- The Moment lives in daylight (a journal; its own serif voice, Lora).
- The Lab gets its own light later; for now it sits in the studio's paper.
- Versos is paper.

Shared by all rooms: the mark, the wordmark, the nav, the credit line,
the words. Owned by each room: its light and its type voice.

## The mark

A square frame whose stroke develops from nothing to full and stops just
short of closing. Nothing inside, because the photograph is already there.
Parent of the products' existing marks (the L that fades in, the ring that
fades in). See design/identity. Open to a better idea.

## The homepage, in order

1. The belief, alone: "The photograph is already there." With two sentences:
   instruments for developing photographs, and places for living with them;
   nothing we make competes with the picture, it accompanies it.
2. What the studio makes: the Ritual, the Moment, the Lab, Versos. Each with
   one image in its own light, a line, a status, a door.
3. One engine, one habit: the passage that makes four things one house.
4. The shared world: Circle, Field, and the wall.
5. Letters. A quiet footer with the founder's name.

## The question to answer with design

What is the concept that makes this a place and not a page? The book has
"a living manuscript". Versos has "a wall of prints". The studio needs its
own: a floor plan, a workshop wall, a contact sheet, a colophon, a set of
doors. Find it, then let every detail serve it. Two or three genuinely
different directions are more useful than five shades of one.

## Constraints

- Astro static site, no framework. Self-hosted fonts only, no external
  requests. Real photographs exist (see public/).
- Mobile must be as considered as desktop.
- The instruments' own pages already exist and are not part of this brief.
