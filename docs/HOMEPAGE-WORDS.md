# latentstudios.art, the homepage in words

Draft 2, 3 September 2026. Words first, design after.

The studio is the home. Four things sit directly under it, each with its
own tab as today: the Ritual, the Moment, the Lab, and Versos. No level in
between. The name "Latent" survives as the studio's name and as the
engine's ("the Latent print engine"), not as a fourth product.

## The belief

*(big, alone, the first frame, the hero image stays)*

The photograph is already there.

Latent Studios makes instruments for developing photographs, and places
for living with them. Nothing we make competes with the picture. It
accompanies it.

## What the studio makes

*(four rooms in a row, one image each, each in its own register)*

**The Ritual** · Mac and iPad
A complete photographic workroom. Gather a body of work, develop the
negative, make the print, return to it over time.
→ Enter the workroom

**The Moment** · iPhone
A photographer's journal. Photographs, places, words, and the days they
belonged to.
→ Open the journal

**The Lab** · DaVinci Resolve and iPhone
The moving-image print engine. The same practice, carried into motion.
→ See the Lab

**Versos** · a prize, once a month
One line. One photograph each, any camera, a line on its verso. A named
photographer chooses. Nothing is counted.
→ See the call

## One engine, one habit

*(the passage that makes four things one studio; replaces "One engine,
three relationships to time")*

Three instruments share one engine: the Moment catches, the Ritual lets
you sit with the work, the Lab carries the process through motion. The
prize shares their habit. A developer that reveals instead of corrects.
A wall where a person looks instead of a machine. Slow down, look again,
let the photograph say what it already knows.

## The shared world

*(kept as it is: Circle, Field, and now the wall)*

Some photographs remain with us. Others are handed on. Keep them in a
Circle, or place them in the Field. Once a month, hang one on the wall.
The rest belongs to whoever stops to look.

## Later, room to grow

The four rooms are a row today. When a fifth thing exists, it joins the
row. The belief at the top and the passage in the middle are written so
they hold for anything the studio makes that keeps the same habit.
