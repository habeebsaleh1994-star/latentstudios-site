# Latent Studios, the film

Third treatment, 4 September 2026. Replaces the two before it.
Source: the founder interview, and the founder's book, *To Pray Is to
Wander About the Meaning of Life* (habibsaleh.com), read in full.

This is the visual identity of the whole studio, not of any product.
Products get their own films later. This one is about what Latent
Studios feels like, sounds like, smells like: thoughts, visions, dreams,
feelings, before they become anything.

## Where it comes from

Three passages in the book say what the studio is better than any brief.

From the Prelude:

> There are moments when the world seems to withdraw from its own noise,
> and something quietly begins to appear.

From *More Sweet Than Bitter*:

> We are prior to our words. Whatever we are, we were that before we
> learned to speak it, and whatever remains most true about us still
> lives there, in that silence before the first syllable. Every
> photograph, every poem, every careful arrangement of colour and sound
> is an attempt to point back toward that place. Not to capture it. To
> confirm that it exists.

> There is a method for growing rhubarb called forcing. The plant is
> raised in total darkness, tended only in brief intervals of
> candlelight. It thrives by reaching toward an absent sun, stretching so
> fast its growth can be heard. Forcing can weaken the crop, but when
> done carefully, it yields something sweeter. Some things grow toward
> what they cannot see, and the growing is not separate from the
> sweetness.

And from *The Unfinished God*: creation staged for an audience that did
not yet exist. A need to be seen that preceded anything capable of
seeing. The making itself was the point.

That is Latent. Prior to the words. Growing in the dark toward a light
it cannot see. Made before there is anyone to see it. The studio is the
place that tends that growth by candlelight, and the witness that did
not exist yet.

## The title

*Prior.* Or *Forcing.* Or no title. The name of the studio is enough.

## What the film is

Sixty seconds. Abstract throughout. Nothing whole, nothing named, no
instrument, no photograph of a place, no painting of a thing. A material
world that behaves like a feeling: darkness, a candle's warmth, things
reaching, the sound of growing, and then arrival.

It is the story of the rhubarb, told without a rhubarb.

## The movements

**I. The noise withdraws** (0:00 to 0:10)
Not black. A dim, grey, faintly humming surface, the world's noise made
visible: a fine restless static, a flicker, something like a screen that
has been left on in an empty room. Slowly it quietens. The static
settles. The hum thins. The grey goes dark, not switched off, but
withdrawn. Silence arrives before anything else does.

**II. Something quietly begins to appear** (0:10 to 0:24)
In the dark, a warmth. Not a flame we can see: the warmth a candle
leaves on a wall. Within it, the first things: a fibre, a filament, a
thread of silver, reaching. Then another. They grow toward the warmth
the way the forced plant grows toward an absent sun, pale, fast, thin,
almost too eager. We hear them: a creak, a tick, the sound of growth
that should be inaudible. The frame fills slowly with reaching forms,
none of them a plant, none of them anything, all of them alive.

**III. The stretching** (0:24 to 0:38)
The cuts begin, and quicken. Materials arrive and pass: dye soaking into
fibre, silver crystallising, halation blooming at an edge, grain
thickening into structure, a line drawing itself. Between them, for a
fraction of a second each, glimpses of the archive come alive and are
gone: a sky, a textile, a hen, a marbled endpaper, a figure in a garden.
These are the world's images, born before anyone made them, pointing
back toward the place. The pace becomes a pulse. The growing can be
heard now as a rhythm. Something large is coming into view, and the
feeling is the one from the interview: curious, excited, a little
frightening.

**IV. Before the first syllable** (0:38 to 0:41)
Everything stops. Dark. Silence. The instant before something is said.
Held for one full breath, which in a film is a very long time.

**V. Sweeter** (0:41 to 0:53)
One shot, slow. From one point, colour and texture and warmth arrive
with full presence and fill the frame: the register of still 12, dye and
light and grain, cream and rose and ochre and sage, a material becoming
alive. It is not an image of anything. It is what it feels like when
what was prior finally exists. The calm half of the feeling. One held
note.

**VI. The house** (0:53 to 1:00)
Paper. The frame draws itself, open at one corner: the studio's one hard
edge, a way of holding what was born before it was made. Then the name.
Then, if there are words at all, the founder's own:

> We are prior to our words.

Cut to paper.

## What it says about the studio, without saying it

- The withdrawing noise: the world before feeling; also everything the
  studio refuses, the finished, the smooth, the corrected.
- The candle's warmth and the reaching: the studio's method, tending a
  growth in the dark, patiently, by hand.
- The archive flashes: the studio contains many arts; all of them point
  back to the same place.
- The pulse: scale. The tide, not the monster.
- The hold: prior to the words. The spiritual experience.
- The arrival: born before the act, and sweeter for the dark.
- The frame: the studio itself.

## Sound

The film is heard as much as seen. Room tone and a faint electrical hum
in I, dying away. The growth in II: creaks, ticks, the almost-sounds of
something stretching, close to the ear. A pulse in III that quickens
with the cuts. Absolute silence in IV. One held warm note in V. Paper
in VI. No music in the usual sense, ever.

## Palette

I: grey, faint static. II: near-dark with candle warmth, pale silver.
III: the materials' own colours passing. IV: dark. V: cream, rose,
ochre, sage, pale indigo, warm silver, from still 12 and the inspiration
folder. VI: paper and ink.

## How it is made

No live footage. The reaching forms of II and the arrival of V are
generated as clips from approved stills through Runway's video models
and, where a generated clip cannot hold the abstraction, drawn in code
with shaders. The archive flashes are the founder's inspiration folder
brought to life, a second each. The structure, timing, pulse, frame,
words and export are code, on the scaffold that exists.

## What is not in it

A product. A screen. A camera. A photograph of a place. A painting of a
thing. A caption before the end. A tagline that belongs to an app.
