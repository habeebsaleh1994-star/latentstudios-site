# Latent Studios, the film

Treatment, 3 September 2026. Thirty seconds, one idea, told once.
Made with the studio's own instruments: shot on a phone or any camera,
developed through the Lab in Resolve. No screenshots, no music bed that
sells. Silence is allowed.

## The reference, watched

The Claude film (27 seconds): bare branches against a pale sky, a small
bird, the moon behind cloud, the name set in a serif over clouds, and
twice a circular vignette, as if through an eyepiece, over an old
engineering drawing and a botanical plate. Real footage, slightly soft,
handheld, unhurried. No product on screen. What it says: we look
closely at the world, and at what people have recorded of it. Its device
is the eyepiece: everything is seen through a lens.

What Latent Studios takes from it: real footage over renders, a serif
name over a photograph, and one recurring device. Ours is not an
eyepiece. Ours is the frame.

## The idea

A photograph is not made. It is found. Everything the studio makes is a
way of finding it. The film shows one photograph being found, four times,
and ends on the frame.

## Voice

One voice, unhurried, close to the microphone, like reading aloud in a
room. The lines are the ones on the site. Nothing new is written for the
film; the film proves the site's words are true.

## Shots

**0:00 · Black.** Not dark grey, black. A breath. Then, at 0:02, the
faintest amber, the way a darkroom's safelight comes up.

**0:02 · The sheet.** A blank sheet of paper in a tray, under red light.
Real, if you can; a Ritual print room render if you can't. Nothing on it.
Hold for three seconds. Longer than is comfortable.

*Voice:* "The photograph is already there."

**0:06 · It arrives.** The image comes up in the developer, slowly, from
nothing. This is the whole film; give it eight seconds. Use one of your
own photographs, the carousel from the homepage or one from the book.
The shot is locked off, no camera move. The only motion is the image
appearing.

*Voice, over the arrival:* "Development does not invent it. It brings
into view what was already latent."

**0:14 · Four ways of finding it.** Quick, but not fast. Four shots of
three seconds each, the same quiet light. Each is seen through the frame:
a thin square, open at one corner, over the footage, the way the
reference looks through its eyepiece. The frame is the studio's device,
and by the end of the film the viewer knows it before it is named:

1. A hand holding a phone up to a window in the morning. The Moment.
   Someone catching something.
2. A desk at night, one lamp, a person sitting with a print in their
   hands, not doing anything to it. The Ritual. Someone sitting with
   the work.
3. Frames of the Lab reel, the girl in the trees, the grain alive,
   held for three seconds, no cut inside. The Lab. The same practice,
   carried into motion.
4. A print pinned to a wall, a name written beneath it in pencil, and
   a hand turning it over to read the back. Versos. Someone looks.

*Voice, one line per shot, unhurried:* "The Moment catches. The Ritual
lets you sit with the work. The Lab carries it through motion. Versos
hands one photograph to someone who looks."

**0:26 · The frame.** Paper. The mark draws itself: a stroke arriving
from nothing, along the top, down the right, along the bottom, up the
left, and stopping just short. Nothing inside it. Two seconds.

**0:28 · The name.** Under the frame, in the studio's serif: Latent
Studios. Beneath it, smaller: instruments for developing photographs,
and places for living with them. Hold. Cut to paper.

## Sound

Room tone throughout. The tray's water in the first shots. A page turning
at the wall. No score until the last four seconds, and then only if it
is one held note. If in doubt, silence.

## Where it lives

- The top of the studio homepage, muted, playing once, with the frame
  and name resolving into the real page beneath it.
- The first post from Latent Studios on Instagram.
- Sent to jurors and sponsors with the Versos draft.

## What can be made in code before a single frame is shot

The last four seconds. The frame drawing itself and the name arriving is
an animation the site can carry on its own: the loader, the favicon's
motion, the share card's video. Build that first; it is the identity's
signature, and the film ends on it.

## What only Habib can make

Everything before it. The sheet in the tray, the hand at the window, the
desk at night, the print on the wall. Real light, real hands, your
photographs. That is the point of the film, and why nobody else could
make it.
