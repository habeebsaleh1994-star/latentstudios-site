# The sample bodies of work

Every template on the home page and the start page runs with a body of work of its own kind, so that a painter sees a painter's site and a poet a poet's. Each is a real body of work from an open collection, shown under the real name, and each sample says so on its About and Contact pages. The arrangement, the page titles and the words about the work are the sample's own. The files are sized to 2400 px, as uploads are.

Each sample folder keeps a `manifest.json` with the source record of every file (accession number, credit line, page on the source site). The documents are written by a builder script and can be rebuilt; the museum's own descriptions appear as pauses and are marked "From the museum".

The Cleveland Museum of Art asks for no credit under CC0 but the samples give one on every work's verso. The Blender films require attribution (CC BY), given in the credits of each film page and on the site's foot. Nothing here is on the live site.
