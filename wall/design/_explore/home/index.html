Three ways to arrive.

The present home is a tidy brochure: it says what Wall is. These are three ways to make the first minute felt instead: abundance, effortlessness, and the certainty that your site will be your own. Every picture here is the real product running; only the words over them are drawn. One direction is chosen, then stills of its screens, then motion.

"He needs to feel there is so much for me to work with and show my identity; that his needs will be fulfilled here effortlessly, without thinking too much. Aesthetically immersive and state of the art, as much variety artistically without repeating itself. Even if a thousand artists bought a website from us they would still have something different and unique."
Direction A

Arrive inside.

No brochure, no box. The visitor lands inside a Wall site, full screen and running, the claim set over it. As they scroll the words stay and the site changes template beneath them: the same body of work, nine identities. "Two artists never look alike" is felt before it is said.

What it says

This is not a tool, it is a place; your work could be on this wall tonight.

What it risks

The first screen is one template; a painter might think "photographs only" until scroll 2. The template changes must be slow and quiet, never a slideshow.

Motion

The site beneath the words cross-fades every few seconds, and advances with the scroll. Everything else still.

Direction B

The thousand walls.

Abundance first. The first screen is a slow drift across a field of real sites, each a different template, look and type, at different scales, like a gallery wall seen from a passing train. One true number sits on it. Then one site grows to fill the screen and the words arrive: find yourself here.

79,328designed ways a site can be set, before its colour, spacing or scale. Nine templates, each with looks and type made for it. No two artists alike.
What it says

There is so much here; whatever I am, one of these is me, and it will not be anyone else's.

What it risks

A field of small screens can read as a catalogue again if it is static or too neat. It must drift slowly, at different depths, with the number set once and large. The number is true (`houses.ts`); it must stay true.

Motion

A continuous slow drift (one pixel a second), parallax between rows; on scroll the field clears and one site grows. The most cinematic of the three.

Direction C

Your name on it.

Effortlessness first. The first screen asks one thing, and nothing else is on it: your name. Type it, and every template that follows is already set in your name, with its words in your name's type. You find yourself in the sites before you have chosen anything; the page is already yours.

What it says

This was made for me; I did not have to think, and it is already mine.

What it risks

Asking before showing: some will not type. The field must be optional (scroll past it and the sites show a quiet default name). The sample work stays ours until they bring theirs; the page must say so lightly.

Motion

Each keystroke re-sets the name on the sites below in the template's own type. Otherwise the same slow template changes as A.

After any arrival: the walk

Five moments, shown, not listed.

A folder becomes a story.

Drop a folder; the story appears in the order the pictures were taken, with the titles and captions in the files.

Turn it over.

A print flips as you pass it: where it was made, a line in the artist's hand, the edition.

A door.

A page meets its word; the files behind it are sealed, not hidden.

Life size.

A work grows to its centimetres on the screen, matched to a bank card.

Publish, and every version kept.

What changed, in your words; put any earlier site back.

Then: who it is for (painter, photographer, filmmaker, writer: one line, one template each); plans in one line; start. The present "Make it yours" lists and three-step section go.

My reading. B answers the brief most directly: the thousand walls are the abundance and the uniqueness, and the number is ours alone to claim. A is the most immersive and the simplest to make flawless. C is the most effortless and personal, and could be the second screen of either. The strongest page may be B's arrival with C's name field placed where the one site grows to fill the screen: "find yourself here", and your name already on it.
