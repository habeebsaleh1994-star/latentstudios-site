Templates

Eighteen ways for work to be seen.

Each template makes the big decisions for you: how its pages are built, how a story moves, the looks and type that suit it. Point at one to see it move; open it to try it with your own work.

Every preview is the real site, running. The work in them is ours, to show how each one holds a body of photographs, a film and a poem.

Make it yours

The template decides. You choose within it.

So you are never flooded with decisions, and two artists never end up with the same site. Every choice is designed, and every one is tested to change the page.

How it works

Three steps, and the work stays yours.

Your work is kept apart from the design around it, so you can change your mind about the design at any time.

One

Choose a template.

Each is a finished design for a kind of work. Start from the one that fits yours; you can change it later.

Two

Add your work.

Drop in your photographs. They live in one library, and one can sit in several stories. Type your words where they will be read.

Three

Publish.

Your site goes live at its own address, and at your own domain when you want one.
