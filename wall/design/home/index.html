How it works

Three steps, and the work stays yours.

Your work is kept apart from the design around it. Change the look, or the template, and nothing you added is lost.

1

Choose a template.

Each one is a finished design made for a kind of work: photographs, writing, film, paintings. Pick the one that suits yours.

2

Add your work.

Drop in your pictures and write your words. Soon you will also be able to send work straight from The Moment and The Ritual.

3

Publish.

Your site goes live at its own address. Add your own domain when you want one.

In design
Make it yours

A look first, then your own choices.

The same template can feel like a gallery, a zine or a darkroom. Every look is designed as a whole, and every combination is tested, so nothing you choose can leave your work hard to read.
