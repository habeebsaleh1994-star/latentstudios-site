# Parked ideas (not now)

Habib, 5 October 2026: wait until the system is stable (templates and looks) before any of this. Do not build these until he says so.

Thesis: other builders start from a blank page; Latent Wall starts from the work, which has already been captured (Moment), developed (Ritual) and printed (Lab).

- **Turn it over.** Every work can turn to its verso: place, date, a handwritten line, the edition. Echoes Moment's "turn the print over" and Versos.
- **The making is part of the work.** Roll, film stock, development, the print's sealed Recipe Card, carried with the work.
- **Looks grown from the artist's own pictures**, plus authored looks credited to their designers, with the same guarantee that nothing can look wrong.
- **Life size.** Show a work at real size from the visitor's screen; on a phone, hang it on their wall in AR.
- **Private view.** A private link with a hang arranged for one curator or buyer, a personal note, and an expiry.
- **No counting, ever.** No likes, ranks or public counts; at most a quiet private "who looked" for the artist.
- **It outlives us.** Plain export of the whole site as ordinary files or a printable book, any time.

Avoid: a template marketplace, free-form canvas editing, analytics dashboards.
