RITUAL FOR THE CANON EOS 5D MARK II
by Latent Studios · www.latentstudios.art
This guide is kept up to date at www.latentstudios.art/firmware/guide

You need: the 5D Mark II with a full battery, a CompactFlash card (8 GB or more), a Mac.
Your photos are always Canon's own raw file (CR2, in DCIM), plus Ritual's photo on
your film, in the RITUAL folder on the card.

Before you start: the camera must have Canon's firmware 2.1.2.
Check it in MENU → Firmware Ver. If it shows another number, install Canon's 2.1.2 from
Canon's website first.


1. MAKE THE CARD (on the Mac)
   - Copy any photos off the card. Put the card in the camera and format it
     (MENU → Format).
   - Put the card in the Mac (a card reader is fine) and open Make Ritual Card.
   - Click "Make the card". It puts Ritual and its films on the card and checks
     everything (a few minutes), then asks for the Mac's password once. It ejects
     the card when it is done.


2. THE FIRST TIME ONLY (in the camera)
   - Put the card in the camera and turn it on.
   - Press MENU, go to Firmware Ver. 2.1.2, press SET, then choose OK.
   - Choose RITSETUP.FIR. The screen says "Set up Ritual on this camera".
     Press SET to set it up (MENU: not now).
   - While it says "Setting up Ritual", do not switch off or take the battery out.
     It takes a few seconds.
   - When it says "Ritual is ready", switch the camera off, then on.
     If it says "Ritual is already set up", nothing needed changing: switch off, then on.
   - The screen goes back to Canon by itself after 12 seconds, or press SET.
   - If it says "This could not finish", nothing was changed: bring the card to us.
     If it says "The camera needs a quick repair", keep the camera switched on and
     contact us.
   - You never need to do this again on this camera.


3. SHOOTING
   - Turn the camera on with the Ritual card in it. The red light comes on for a
     moment while Ritual starts. The back screen stays dark while you shoot.
   - Press INFO to see Ritual's film screen. Turn the dial to choose a film,
     press SET to load it.
   - Shoot as usual. A few seconds after each shot its picture is ready on the
     back screen, on the film you shot it on. Press INFO, or PLAY, to see your photos.
   - Every photo is also saved on your film at a share size (2040 x 1360), made
     quietly in the background. The bottom of the photo screen says what is
     happening: "Share size next", "Share size developing", then
     "Share size · SET for full size".
   - For the full size (2808 x 1872) of one photo: press SET on it. The screen says
     "Full size next", "Full size developing", then "Full size ready".
   - For every photo at the full size: on the film screen, press ▼ to the Size row
     and turn the dial to Full (Share to go back). It stays as you leave it.
   - On a cine film (Kodak Vision3 250D or 500T) the film screen has a Halation
     row: the red glow around lights, as the film has it without its backing.
     Press ▼ to it and turn the dial: Soft, Medium or Full. It stays as you leave it.
     The photo shows first without it; the glowing one takes its place when its
     share size is ready.
   - To delete a photo: press the trash button on it, then SET.


4. MORE FILMS LATER
   - When there is a new Ritual, put your Ritual card in the Mac, open the new
     Make Ritual Card and click "Make the card". Your photos stay.


5. WORDS ON THE SCREEN
   Film         The film each photo develops on. Turn the dial to choose, press
                SET to load it.
   Development  "Box speed", or the film pushed or pulled: developed longer or
                shorter, as a lab does. Pushed: more contrast and grain.
                Pulled: softer.
   Paper        The paper the photo is printed on. Some black-and-white papers
                also have a Grade (0 to 5): the higher, the more contrast.
   Halation     Cine films only: the red glow around bright lights. Soft,
                Medium or Full.
   Light        The light Ritual develops for: your camera's white balance
                (Auto shows the last light it measured, e.g. 4000 K).
   Roll         Ritual counts your photos in rolls of 36, like film:
                "Roll 3 · 12 of 36". A new roll starts by itself after 36,
                or when you load another film.
   Size         Share size (2040 x 1360, quicker, for phones) or Full size
                (2808 x 1872). Press SET on a photo to make its full size.
   Developing   The photo being made now. Its picture shows first, then its
                share size goes into the RITUAL folder.


6. HOW LONG IT TAKES
   - A photo's picture on the screen: about 15 seconds.
   - Its share size in the RITUAL folder: about 35 seconds more (a minute in
     all, a cine film with its glow too). A full size takes a little longer.
   - After a burst they develop one by one, the newest first. You can look at
     them in PLAY meanwhile. Switched off before they are all done? They go on
     the next time you turn the camera on: nothing is lost.


7. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
   - INFO is the key button. It opens Ritual's film screen, and it shows your
     photos as they develop. If a photo hasn't appeared yet, press INFO.
   - Sometimes the first photo after you turn the camera on doesn't show until
     you take another one. Take a second photo and both appear.
   - The camera freezes (the buttons do nothing, or the screen is stuck): wait
     until the red card light is off, take the battery out for a few seconds,
     put it back and turn the camera on. Nothing is lost: what was developing
     goes on.
   - Never take the card or the battery out while the red card light is on.
   - A photo does not show yet: press INFO or PLAY. A cine photo shows first
     without its glow; the glowing one takes its place when its share size
     is ready.
   - The back screen stays dark while you shoot: that is Ritual, as it should be.
   - "Reading the card" when you turn the camera on: wait a few seconds.
   - Still stuck? Write to us (www.latentstudios.art). The card keeps a log of
     what happened: we may ask you for it.

Do not format the Ritual card in the camera unless you want Ritual off it
(you can always make it again with step 1).
With a normal card, the camera works exactly like a normal Canon.

The films work only inside Ritual on the camera, and only with this kit.
