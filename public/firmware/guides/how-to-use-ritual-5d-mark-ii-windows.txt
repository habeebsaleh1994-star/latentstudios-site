RITUAL FOR THE CANON EOS 5D MARK II
by Latent Studios · www.latentstudios.art

You need: the 5D Mark II with a full battery, a real CompactFlash card (8 GB or more;
an SD or microSD card in an adapter may not work), and a Windows PC (Windows 10 or 11).
Your photos are always Canon's own raw file (CR2, in DCIM), plus Ritual's photo on
your film, in the RITUAL folder on the card.

Before you start: the camera must have Canon's firmware 2.1.2.
Check it in MENU → Firmware Ver. If it shows another number, install Canon's 2.1.2 from
Canon's website first.


1. MAKE THE CARD (on the PC)
   - Copy any photos off the card. Put the card in the camera and format it
     (MENU → Format).
   - Put the card in the PC (a card reader is fine). Open the folder you
     downloaded (a zip: right-click it, choose Extract All, open the new
     folder) and open Make Ritual Card. If Windows says "Windows protected
     your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
     Open it from your computer, never from the card.
   - Add your films: download the ones you want at latentstudios.art (each film
     is a file of its own), then click "Add films…" and choose them, or drag them
     onto the window. They stay on this computer for next time.
   - Tick the films for this card: the fewer films, the more room for photos
     (it says how many will fit).
   - Click "Make the card". It puts Ritual and its films on the card and checks
     everything (a few minutes), then Windows asks once to allow it to mark
     the card: click Yes. It ejects
     the card when it is done.


2. THE FIRST TIME ONLY (in the camera)
   - Put the card in the camera and turn it on.
   - Press MENU, go to Firmware Ver. 2.1.2, press SET, then choose OK. There is
     no file to choose: the camera finds it on the card by itself.
   - The screen says "Set up Ritual on this camera". Press SET to set it up
     (MENU: not now).
   - While it says "Setting up Ritual", do not switch off or take the battery out.
     It takes a few seconds.
   - When it says "Ritual is ready", switch the camera off, then on.
     If it says "Ritual is already set up", nothing needed changing: switch off, then on.
   - The screen goes back to Canon by itself after 12 seconds, or press SET.
   - If it says "This could not finish", nothing was changed: bring the card to us.
     If it says "The camera needs a quick repair", keep the camera switched on and
     contact us.
   - You never need to do this again on this camera.


3. SHOOTING
   - Set the camera to RAW (MENU → Quality). RAW + JPEG works too.
   - Turn the camera on with the Ritual card in it. The red light comes on for a
     moment while Ritual starts. The back screen stays dark while you shoot.
   - Press INFO to see Ritual's film screen, from anywhere in the camera. Turn the
     dial to choose a film, press SET to load it.
   - Shoot as usual. A few seconds after each shot its picture is ready on the
     back screen, on the film you shot it on. Press INFO to see your photos.
     PLAY is Canon's own playback (your raws), as on any 5D Mark II.
   - Leave the camera on while your photos develop. It stays awake by itself until
     they are done, then sleeps as usual. Canon's own playback and menus pause the
     developing; it carries on when you come back.
   - Every photo is also saved on your film at a share size (2040 x 1360), made
     quietly in the background. The bottom of the photo screen says what is
     happening: "Share size next", "Share size developing", then
     "Share size · SET for full size".
   - For the full size (2808 x 1872) of one photo: press SET on it. The screen says
     "Full size next", "Full size developing", then "Full size ready".
   - For every photo at the full size: on the film screen, press ▼ to the Size row
     and turn the dial to Full (Share to go back). It stays as you leave it.
   - On a cine film (Kodak Vision3 250D or 500T) the film screen has a Halation
     row: the red glow around lights, as the film has it without its backing.
     Press ▼ to it and turn the dial: Soft, Medium or Full. It stays as you leave it.
     The photo shows first without it; the glowing one takes its place when its
     share size is ready.
   - To delete a photo: press the trash button on it, then SET. Delete photos in
     the camera, not on a computer: the card keeps what belongs together.


4. A NEW RITUAL, MORE FILMS
   - A new Ritual (from 1.0 too): the same card, made again. Delete the old
     download from your computer, copy your photos off your Ritual card, format
     that card in the camera (MENU → Format), then make it with the new Make
     Ritual Card and its films, as in step 1. The first time (step 2) is not needed again on a
     camera that has Ritual; doing it again does no harm.
   - To add a film: download it, add it ("Add films…"), tick it and click "Make
     the card". To take one off, untick it: it is taken off the card.


5. WORDS ON THE SCREEN
   Film         The film each photo develops on. Turn the dial to choose, press
                SET to load it.
   Development  "Box speed", or the film pushed or pulled: developed longer or
                shorter, as a lab does. Pushed: more contrast and grain.
                Pulled: softer.
   Paper        The paper the photo is printed on. Some black-and-white papers
                also have a Grade (0 to 5): the higher, the more contrast.
   Halation     Cine films only: the red glow around bright lights. Soft,
                Medium or Full.
   Light        The light Ritual develops for: your camera's white balance
                (Auto shows the last light it measured, e.g. 4000 K).
   Roll         Like a film camera's frame counter: "Roll 3 · frame 12 next" is
                the frame your next shot will be. Loading another film (or
                development) starts a new roll at frame 1; after 36 frames the
                screen says "Roll 3 finished · Roll 4 next". Each photo says the
                roll and frame it was shot on. It is never a count of photos.
                Each roll is its own folder in RITUAL (001_T500: roll 1, its
                film), its photos named by frame, with its index print
                (INDEX.JPG): the whole roll on one sheet.
   Size         Share size (2040 x 1360, quicker, for phones) or Full size
                (2808 x 1872). Press SET on a photo to make its full size.
   Developing   The photo being made now. Its picture shows first, then its
                share size goes into the RITUAL folder.


6. HOW LONG IT TAKES
   - A photo's picture on the screen: a few seconds (about 7).
   - Its share size in the RITUAL folder: about 40 seconds more (cine films with
     their glow a little longer). A full size takes about 40 seconds more again.
   - After a burst they develop one by one, the newest first. You can look at
     them in INFO meanwhile; Canon's own PLAY pauses the developing until you
     close it. Switched off before they are all done? They go on
     the next time you turn the camera on: nothing is lost.


7. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
   - The camera freezes (the buttons do nothing, the screen is stuck, or "Err"):
     take the battery out for a few seconds, even if the red card light stays
     on, put it back and turn the camera on. Nothing is lost: your raws are
     safe, and what was developing goes on.
   - While the camera works normally, wait for the red card light to go off
     before you take the card out.
   - A photo does not show yet: press INFO. A cine photo shows first
     without its glow; the glowing one takes its place when its share size
     is ready.
   - The back screen stays dark while you shoot: that is Ritual, as it should be.
   - "Reading the card" when you turn the camera on: wait a few seconds.
   - Still stuck? Write to us (www.latentstudios.art). The card keeps a log of
     what happened: we may ask you for it.

Do not format the Ritual card in the camera unless you want Ritual off it
(you can always make it again with step 1).
With a normal card, the camera works exactly like a normal Canon.

TO TURN RITUAL OFF FOR GOOD: format a card in the camera, copy RITUNSET.FIR from
the "Turn Ritual off" folder onto it (nothing else), and run it from MENU →
Firmware Ver. as in step 2. The screen says "Switch Ritual off on this camera":
press SET. When it says "Ritual is off", switch the camera off, then on.
